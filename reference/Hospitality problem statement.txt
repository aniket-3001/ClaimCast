Precision Care Challenge 2026
Hospitality: Holistic Optimization System for Policy-Integrated
Admission & Treatment Intelligence
Problem Statement
A large majority of individuals today are covered by one or more health insurance schemes. The
government has also rolled out a number of health provisions in the form of schemes like ESI, PM-JAY,
Arogya Karnataka, Yeshaswini etc.. This is beyond the private/employer insurance schemes. However,
during medical emergencies or hospital admissions, patients and caregivers often face significant
confusion due to lack of timely, structured information. Critical questions, such as which hospitals are
covered, what type of room is eligible, what procedures or consumables are reimbursable, and what
alternatives exist, are frequently answered too late, resulting in financial stress, delayed care, or
suboptimal decisions.
Insurance policy details, hospital capabilities, and treatment progress information typically exist
across disconnected documents, portals, and manual processes. Moreover, this information is not
readily available to caregivers and is mostly available with the patients themselves. Patients are
required to interpret complex policy language while simultaneously managing medical stress, leading
to avoidable errors and inefficiencies.
There is a need for a unified India-specific solution that consolidates insurance policy information,
publicly available hospital data, and treatment progress signals to assist patients and caregivers in
making informed, real-time decisions during hospital admission and care.
Challenge Objective
The objective of this challenge is to design and build a system that helps users navigate hospital care
using their insurance coverage as a guiding constraint.
The platform should:
● Map a userʼs insurance details to hospital options and treatment pathways.
● Suggest eligible hospitals, room categories, and care alternatives based on available data.
● Track the patientʼs treatment journey and continuously surface relevant insurance-aware
guidance.
The solution must function strictly as a decision-support and information platform and must not
provide medical diagnoses, clinical treatment recommendations, or binding insurance advice.
Guidelines to the Solution Capabilities:
The following is an indicative set of features that could be considered while building a solution to
alleviate this issue. However, participants are urged to think creatively and beyond the suggested
guidelines below.
● Insurance and User Data Ingestion
o Allow users to input or upload structured insurance details such as: Insurer name,
policy type, coverage limits, room eligibility, exclusions.
o Normalize policy information into a standardized internal representation.
o Use only synthetic or user-provided mock data for insurance details.
● Hospital and Care Option Mapping
o Integrate publicly available or simulated hospital datasets, including, the hospital
location, specialties, room types, indicative costs, and network status.
o Map insurance constraints (e.g., room category eligibility, network hospitals) to
hospital options.
o Suggest suitable hospitals and room categories based on user constraints and context.
● Care Journey Tracking and Contextual Suggestions
o Track the high-level progress of a patientʼs hospital journey (e.g., admission,
investigation, procedure, recovery).
o At each stage, surface context-aware information such as:
▪ Expected insurance coverage for the current phase.
▪ Possible alternatives (e.g., room downgrade/upgrade implications, transfer
options).
o Provide descriptive explanations for all suggestions using clear, non-technical
language.
● User-Facing Experience
o Provide a simple web-based interface for patients or caregivers.
o Display:
▪ Insurance summary and key constraints.
▪ Suggested hospitals and room categories.
▪ A timeline view of the care journey with informational prompts.
o Ensure usability for non-expert users under stressful conditions.
Data Guidance
No data will be provided from GE HealthCare and will rely on participantsʼ enterprise to obtain
relevant information.
Evaluation Criteria
● A convincing vision of the solution, and clear pathway to implement it. (20%)
● A working solution, even if scope is narrow (30%)
● Innovative use of AI to solve practical problems (30%)
● Enterprise in understanding problem, sourcing data, presentation skills etc. (20%)
